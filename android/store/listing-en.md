## Full description (max 4000)
LogistiTrack is a transport operations platform for logistics companies that want one clear view of their daily work.

Built for dispatchers, company managers and drivers, LogistiTrack connects the office and the road:

• OPERATIONS OVERVIEW
See active transports, upcoming departures, fleet readiness, delivered transports, cargo in transit and open incidents on one dashboard.

• TRANSPORTS WITHOUT THE CHAOS
Create a transport, assign a driver and vehicle, attach cargo and follow every status from loading to signed delivery. The optional route measurement fills in the driving distance and duration automatically.

• FLEET AND CARGO
Keep vehicles, registrations, capacities and ownership in one place. Register cargo with consignor, consignee, weight, volume and handling instructions, then attach it to a transport.

• DRIVER APP
Drivers see only their own transports. They can start the route and open Google Maps driving directions, advance loading, transit and unloading status, record CMR and proof of delivery, and report incidents with location and severity.

• TEAM INVITATIONS
Managers invite drivers by email or with a secure shareable link. Drivers choose their own password — managers never need to know it. Accounts can be blocked or removed at any time.

• DISPATCH MESSAGES
A simple conversation between the company and each driver keeps updates in context.

• COMPANY ACCESS, ISOLATED BY DESIGN
Each company has its own workspace. Managers only see their company's fleet, cargo, transports, drivers and tickets. The platform administrator has read-only oversight of company activity, plus company verification and suspension controls.

• SUPPORT
Companies and drivers can raise support tickets and follow the resolution.

Available in English and Bosnian.

LogistiTrack is a business tool. No advertising. No in-app purchases. Internet connection required.
