Privacy Policy

How LogistiTrack handles company, driver and transport data.

Who we are

LogistiTrack is a transport operations platform used by logistics companies and their drivers. The platform is operated by the LogistiTrack team. For privacy questions, contact certidevelopment@gmail.com.

Data we process

  • Account data: company name, legal and tax details, manager and driver names, usernames, email addresses, phone numbers and licence numbers.
  • Transport data: routes, addresses, dates, distances, vehicle registration, cargo details, status history, proof of delivery and CMR numbers.
  • Operational messages: messages between a company's dispatch team and its drivers, incidents and support tickets.
  • Technical data: sign-in timestamps, IP address and standard server logs needed to operate and secure the service.

Why we process it

We process this data to provide the transport management service, authenticate users, keep companies' data separate, calculate route distances, send driver invitations and password links, provide support and protect the platform from abuse. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Company responsibility

Each company is responsible for the data it enters about its drivers, customers, consignors and consignees, and for having a lawful basis to share it with LogistiTrack. Company managers control their own drivers, cargo, vehicles and transports.

Service providers

  • Microsoft SQL Server hosting for the database.
  • Cloudflare Pages for the web application delivery.
  • Google Gmail SMTP for transactional email (invitations, password links, test messages).
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim/Photon and OSRM for geocoding and route distance. Only the place names entered for a route are sent for this calculation.

Security and retention

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and passwords are stored as BCrypt hashes. Access is restricted by company and role. We keep operational data while the company account is active, then delete or anonymise it on request, unless a legal obligation requires longer retention. Backups are kept for a limited period.

Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your personal data, or object to processing, by contacting certidevelopment@gmail.com. Drivers can also ask their company manager to correct or remove their account.

Account deletion

To delete your account and related personal data, follow the instructions at logistic-management-ui.pages.dev/delete-account.html or email us. Company accounts are deleted together with their operational data after verification.

Children

LogistiTrack is a business tool and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

We may update this policy when the service changes. The current version is always available at this address.
