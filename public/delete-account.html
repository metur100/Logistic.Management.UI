Delete your account

You can request deletion of your LogistiTrack account and the personal data associated with it.

Drivers

Drivers can ask their company manager to remove their account. Managers can do this from Drivers → Remove in the company workspace. The driver account is disabled and personal sign-in data is removed; transport history required for company records may remain associated with the company.

Managers and companies

A company manager can request deletion of the entire company workspace. Because this removes the company's transports, cargo, fleet, drivers, messages and support history, we verify ownership before processing it.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us from the address associated with your account, or include your company name and username.
  2. Use the subject Account deletion request.
  3. We confirm the request and process it within 30 days. We may ask for one verification step to protect the account.
Email account deletion request
Data needed for legal, tax or fraud-prevention obligations may be retained for the required period. Backups are purged on the normal backup rotation.

What is deleted

  • Account profile, email, phone, licence number and login credentials.
  • For a company: company profile, drivers, vehicles, cargo, transports, messages, incidents and support tickets.
  • Sessions are invalidated immediately.
